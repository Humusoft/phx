phx.SoftBody

Deformable body made of a triangle mesh

Class  phx.SoftBody  |  Superclass: phx.base.Object

Description

phx.SoftBody is a mesh whose vertices are particles of equal mass held together by its edges: a cloth if the mesh is open, a cushion or a ball if it is closed, optionally inflated by a gas pressure. It collides with rigid bodies (phx.Body), not with other soft bodies. No joint can hold it directly: a body welded into it with phx.IntersectionWeld holds and loads it instead.

The mesh is the rest shape, placed at Position and Orientation when the simulation creates the soft body. The rest shape and its material are read once, at that moment; the settings in the second group of properties apply at any time. A soft body is a top-level object: hand it to the simulation like a body.

Creation

Syntax

soft = phx.SoftBody(ax,"Vertices",V,"Faces",F) soft = phx.SoftBody(___,Name,Value)

Description

soft = phx.SoftBody(ax,"Vertices",V,"Faces",F) creates a soft body of the triangle mesh V (N×3, in the body frame) and F (M×3 vertex indices, as for patch) and draws it into the axes ax; [] draws nothing. A soft body without a mesh is an error.

Properties

Rest shape and material (read when the simulation starts)

PropertyType / DefaultDescription
VerticesN×3 doubleVertices of the mesh in the body frame.
FacesM×3 doubleTriangles of the mesh as vertex indices. A closed mesh holding a Pressure lists them counter-clockwise seen from outside.
Position[0 0 0]Position of the rest shape when the soft body is created, m.
Orientationeye(3)Orientation of the rest shape when the soft body is created.
Mass1Total mass, spread evenly over the vertices, kg.
Compliance0Compliance of the edges, the inverse of their stiffness, m/N; 0 keeps them at their rest length.
BendComplianceInfCompliance of the bend edges across each pair of adjacent triangles, m/N; Inf leaves them out.

Settings (apply at any time)

PropertyType / DefaultDescription
Pressure0Gas pressure inside a closed mesh as n·R·T, J: it pushes on the faces with this value over the enclosed volume.
VertexRadius0Radius of the spheres the vertices collide as, m.
Iterations5Solver substeps of the soft body per simulation step.
Damping0Damping of the vertex velocities, dv/dt = −Damping·v, 1/s.
Friction0.5Friction coefficient; a contact combines the values of both bodies.
Restitution0Restitution (bounciness) coefficient, 0 = no bounce.
Color1×3 doubleColor of the drawn mesh.
CurrentVerticesN×3 double (read-only)Present vertex positions in the global frame: the deformed mesh while the simulation runs, the placed rest shape before.

Examples

An inflated ball dropped on the floor

clf; view(3); axis equal; grid on; camlight headlight;

floor = phx.Body("Type", "static", "Position", [0 0 -0.1], "Shape", {"Box", "Size", [3 3 0.2]});
[x, y, z] = sphere(16);
V = unique([x(:) y(:) z(:)]*0.3, "rows");
F = convhull(V);                               % outward, counter-clockwise
ball = phx.SoftBody(gca, "Vertices", V, "Faces", F, "Position", [0 0 1], ...
    "Compliance", 1e-4, "Pressure", 200);

sim = phx.Simulation({floor, ball});
sim.step(3, 600, 6);
