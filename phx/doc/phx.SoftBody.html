phx.SoftBody

Deformable body made of a triangle mesh

Class  phx.SoftBody  |  Superclass: phx.base.Object

Description

phx.SoftBody is a mesh whose vertices are particles of equal mass held together by its edges: a cloth if the mesh is open, a cushion or a ball if it is closed, optionally inflated by a gas pressure. It collides with rigid bodies (phx.Body), not with other soft bodies. No joint can hold it directly: a body welded into it with phx.IntersectionWeld holds and loads it instead.

The mesh is the rest shape, placed at Position and Orientation when the simulation creates the soft body, its vertices moving at their Velocities. The rest shape, the initial velocities and the material are read once, at that moment; the settings in the second group of properties apply at any time. CurrentVertices and CurrentVelocities read the present state: during a run that of the engine, after it the last one, which stays until the rest shape changes. A new simulation, and a body loaded from a MAT file, start again from the rest shape. A soft body is a top-level object: hand it to the simulation like a body.

Creation

Syntax

soft = phx.SoftBody(ax,"Vertices",V,"Faces",F) soft = phx.SoftBody(ax,"Shape",shape) soft = phx.SoftBody(___,Name,Value)

Description

soft = phx.SoftBody(ax,"Vertices",V,"Faces",F) creates a soft body of the triangle mesh V (N×3, in the body frame) and F (M×3 vertex indices, as for patch) and draws it into the axes ax; [] draws nothing. A soft body without a mesh is an error.

soft = phx.SoftBody(ax,"Shape",shape) covers a shape with the surface mesh it generates (generateSurfaceMesh), its vertices spaced about evenly so that the mass spreads evenly over the surface. The shape is a phx.shape.* object or a cell array of a shape name and its arguments, as phx.Body takes it; its ElementResolution sets how fine the mesh is. Only the geometry is taken: the Mass stays that of the soft body. Box, Sphere, Cylinder, Cone, Capsule and Tube generate meshes; see Meshes from shapes.

Examples

An inflated ball dropped on the floor

clf; view(3); axis equal; grid on; camlight headlight;

floor = phx.Body("Type", "static", "Position", [0 0 -0.1], "Shape", {"Box", "Size", [3 3 0.2]});
ball = phx.SoftBody(gca, "Shape", {"Sphere", "Diameter", 0.6, "ElementResolution", 16}, ...
    "Position", [0 0 1], "Compliance", 1e-4, "Pressure", 200);

sim = phx.Simulation({floor, ball});
sim.step(3, 600, 6);

A mesh of your own: a sphere scaled into an ellipsoid

[V, F] = phx.shape.Sphere("Diameter", 2, "ElementResolution", 16).generateSurfaceMesh;
bag = phx.SoftBody(gca, "Vertices", V.*[0.15 0.3 0.3], "Faces", F, "Pressure", 0.3);
