phx.shape.Sphere

Sphere shape

Class  phx.shape.Sphere  |  Superclass: phx.base.Shape

Description

phx.shape.Sphere is a sphere geometry. The visual appearance is a meridian/parallel grid with an adjustable level of detail; the collision shape and mass properties are modeled as an ideal sphere independent of the visual detail.

A texture is mapped as a standard 2:1 equirectangular image: its top row at the +z pole, its left and right edges on the +x meridian, and longitude growing anticlockwise about +z.

Creation

Syntax

shape = phx.shape.Sphere shape = phx.shape.Sphere(Name,Value)

Description

shape = phx.shape.Sphere creates a unit sphere with default parameters. phx.shape.Sphere(Name,Value) sets properties with name-value arguments.

Properties

Geometry

PropertyType / DefaultDescription
Diameter1Sphere diameter, m.
Radiusdouble (dependent)Sphere radius, m. Setting it updates Diameter.
Segments28Number of meridian segments (level of visual detail); the parallels follow at the same angular step. A multiple of 4 puts a parallel on the equator and meridians on both horizontal axes.
Density1000Volumetric density, kg/m³.

Appearance properties are inherited from phx.base.Shape.

Examples

Simple falling sphere

phx.extra.Viewer("clear", "DefaultCameraPosition", [10 10 10]);

b = phx.Body("Position", [0 0 3], ...
    "Shape", {"Sphere", "Diameter", 0.5, "Density", 2700});

sim = phx.Simulation;
sim.step(2, 200, 1);

A textured ball

phx.extra.Viewer("clear", "DefaultCameraPosition", [10 10 10]);

b = phx.Body("Shape", {"Sphere", "Diameter", 2, "Segments", 48, "Texture", "peppers.png"});

sim = phx.Simulation(gca, "Gravity", [0 0 0]);
b.AngularVelocity = [0 0 0.8];
sim.step(6, 600, 1);
