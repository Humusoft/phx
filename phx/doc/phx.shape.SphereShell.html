phx.shape.SphereShell

Spherical shell shape with a wall of given thickness

Class  phx.shape.SphereShell  |  Superclass: phx.base.Shape

Description

phx.shape.SphereShell is a hollow sphere: a wall of WallThickness inside the outer Diameter, solid from both sides like a vessel of sheet metal. A body outside the shell touches its outer face, a body inside it, in the cavity, its inner face: a static shell holds bodies, particles and liquids inside, and a dynamic one is a hollow ball that rolls and can carry a load inside.

The collision shape is an ideal spherical shell, independent of the visual details, built into the engine: it collides with all other shapes, meshes, terrains and other shells included, as cheaply as a sphere, far cheaper than an inverted triangle mesh would. A convex body in the cavity touches the wall with every corner of its face towards it, so a box rests there on its corners. The shell has no continuous collision detection: a body that passes through the wall within one step, faster than the wall thickness plus its own size per step, goes through it. Mass properties are those of the ideal shell and Density.

The shape draws its outer face and its inner face, turned to the cavity, each a latitude-longitude grid of Segments meridians; a texture is mapped onto both as onto a phx.shape.Sphere. Alpha is the opacity of the wall as a whole: a line of sight through the wall crosses both faces, so each face is drawn with the opacity 1 - sqrt(1 - Alpha). A see-through wall shows what the shell holds.

Creation

Syntax

shape = phx.shape.SphereShell shape = phx.shape.SphereShell(Name,Value)

Description

shape = phx.shape.SphereShell creates a shell of 1 m outer diameter with a 5 cm wall. phx.shape.SphereShell(Name,Value) sets properties with name-value arguments.

Properties

Geometry

PropertyType / DefaultDescription
Diameter1Outer diameter of the shell, m; positive. A phx.shape.Sphere of the same Diameter has the same outside.
Radiusdouble (dependent)Outer radius, m. Setting it updates Diameter.
WallThickness0.05Thickness of the wall, inwards from the outer diameter, m; positive and less than the outer radius.
InnerDiameterdouble (dependent, read-only)Diameter of the cavity, Diameter - 2*WallThickness, m.
Segments28Number of meridian segments of each face; the parallels follow at the same angular step.
Density1000Volumetric density of the wall, kg/m³.

Appearance properties, Alpha among them, are inherited from phx.base.Shape.

Note. WallThickness is checked against the diameter where the shape is used (drawn, weighed or simulated), not when it is set, so the two may be set in any order. A wall as thick as the radius leaves no cavity and is an error (phx:SphereShell:badWallThickness); a solid ball is a phx.shape.Sphere.

Examples

Particles in a see-through tank

phx.extra.Viewer("clear", "DefaultCameraPosition", [2.5 -2.5 2]);

phx.Body("Type", "static", "Position", [0 0 0.6], "Shape", ...
    {"SphereShell", "Diameter", 1.2, "WallThickness", 0.04, "Alpha", 0.4});
phx.Particles(gca, "Shape", {"Box", "Size", [0.5 0.5 0.3]}, ...
    "Position", [0 0 0.7], "Radius", 0.02);

sim = phx.Simulation;
sim.step(3, 600, 1);
