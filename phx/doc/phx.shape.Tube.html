phx.shape.Tube

Tube, pipe or funnel shape

Class  phx.shape.Tube  |  Superclass: phx.base.Shape

Description

phx.shape.Tube is a straight or tapered tube with an open bore. InnerDiameter gives the bore at the negative and the positive end of Axis: a single value d is [d d], a straight tube, and two values taper the bore while the wall keeps its thickness. A zero closes the bore at that end. A single shape thus covers a pipe, a funnel, a hopper and a closed cup. The bore and the outer size read back as pairs.

The collision shape is selected by Envelope. The default "concave" triangle mesh is the only one that leaves the bore open, so it is what a tube that has to hold or pass anything needs; "convex" uses the convex hull of the mesh and seals the tube shut, while a bounding "cylinder" keeps a rolling body smooth where a faceted hull would not. Mass properties are always computed from the actual triangular mesh and Density.

Creation

Syntax

shape = phx.shape.Tube shape = phx.shape.Tube(Name,Value)

Description

shape = phx.shape.Tube creates a unit tube with default parameters. phx.shape.Tube(Name,Value) sets properties with name-value arguments.

Properties

Geometry

PropertyType / DefaultDescription
InnerDiameter[0.9 0.9]Diameters of the bore at the negative and the positive end of Axis, m. A single value d is [d d]; a zero closes that end, leaving a disc as wide as the wall is thick.
InnerRadiusdouble (dependent)Radii of the bore at the two ends, m, as InnerDiameter. Setting it updates InnerDiameter.
WallThickness0.05Thickness of the tube wall, m; positive.
Diameterdouble (dependent)Outer diameters at the two ends, m, that is InnerDiameter plus two walls. Setting it keeps the wall and updates the bore.
Radiusdouble (dependent)Outer radii at the two ends, m. Setting it updates Diameter.
Height1Tube height along the modeling axis, m.
Axis"z"Modeling axis: "x", "y" or "z".
Segments24Number of segments around the circumference.
Density1000Volumetric density, kg/m³.
Envelope"concave"Collision envelope: "concave" triangle mesh, "convex" hull, or bounding "cylinder" about the tube axis. The cylinder takes its radius from the wider outer end and its half height from Height, centred on the shape origin.

Appearance properties are inherited from phx.base.Shape.

Note. The origin of the shape stays at the middle of its axial span, which keeps the shape from shifting in the axes when the bore is changed, as with phx.shape.Revolution. The centre of mass of a tapered tube lies off it, towards the heavier end; the body gets it as its CenterOfMass and turns about it.

Examples

Ball caught by a funnel

phx.extra.Viewer("clear", "DefaultCameraPosition", [3 3 2]);

phx.Body("Type", "static", "Position", [0 0 1], ...
    "Shape", {"Tube", "InnerDiameter", [0.18 0.9], "Height", 0.8});
phx.Body("Position", [0.1 0 2], "Shape", {"Sphere", "Diameter", 0.3});

sim = phx.Simulation;
sim.step(3, 600, 1);
