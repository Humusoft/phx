phx.shape.Tube

Tube, pipe or funnel shape

Class  phx.shape.Tube  |  Superclasses: phx.base.Shape, phx.base.ShapeMesh

Description

phx.shape.Tube is a straight or tapered tube with an open bore. Taper narrows the bore towards one end while the wall keeps its thickness, so a single shape covers a pipe, a funnel, a hopper and a closed cup.

The collision shape is selected by Envelope. The default "concave" triangle mesh is the only one that leaves the bore open, so it is what a tube that has to hold or pass anything needs; "convex" uses the convex hull of the mesh and seals the tube shut, while a bounding "cylinder" keeps a rolling body smooth where a faceted hull would not. Mass properties are always computed from the actual triangular mesh and Density.

Creation

Syntax

shape = phx.shape.Tube shape = phx.shape.Tube(Name,Value)

Description

shape = phx.shape.Tube creates a unit tube with default parameters. phx.shape.Tube(Name,Value) sets properties with name-value arguments.

Properties

Geometry

PropertyType / DefaultDescription
InnerDiameter0.9Inner diameter of the bore at the wide end, m.
InnerRadiusdouble (dependent)Inner radius at the wide end, m. Setting it updates InnerDiameter.
WallThickness0.05Thickness of the tube wall, m.
Diameterdouble (dependent)Outer diameter, m, that is InnerDiameter plus two walls. Setting it keeps the wall and updates the bore.
Radiusdouble (dependent)Outer radius, m. Setting it updates Diameter.
Height1Tube height along the modeling axis, m.
Taper0Taper of the bore, −1 to 1. Zero is a straight tube; a positive value narrows the bore at the end on the positive side of Axis and a negative value at the opposite end. The magnitude is the fraction by which the inner radius shrinks, so 0.3 leaves a bore 70 % as wide and 1 closes it completely. The wall keeps its thickness all along.
Axis"z"Modeling axis: "x", "y" or "z".
Segments24Number of segments around the circumference.
Density1000Volumetric density, kg/m³.
Envelope"concave"Collision envelope: "concave" triangle mesh, "convex" hull, or bounding "cylinder" about the tube axis. The cylinder takes its radius from the wide end and its half height from Height, centred on the shape origin.

Appearance properties are inherited from phx.base.ShapeMesh and skeletal properties from phx.base.Shape.

Note. The origin of the shape stays at the middle of its axial span, not at its centre of mass, so a strongly tapered tube pivots about its geometric centre. This matches phx.shape.Revolution and keeps the shape from shifting in the axes when Taper is changed.

Examples

Ball caught by a funnel

phx.extra.Viewer("clear", "DefaultCameraPosition", [3 3 2]);

phx.Body("Type", "static", "Position", [0 0 1], ...
    "Shape", {"Tube", "Taper", -0.8, "Height", 0.8});
phx.Body("Position", [0.1 0 2], "Shape", {"Sphere", "Diameter", 0.3});

sim = phx.Simulation;
sim.step(3, 600, 1);
