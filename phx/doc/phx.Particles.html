phx.Particles

Group of sphere particles simulated and drawn in bulk

Class  phx.Particles  |  Superclass: phx.base.Object

Description

phx.Particles is a group of small spheres of one radius and density: sand, gravel, grain, beads. They collide with all bodies, and with each other, as bodies do, but the group reads and draws them all at once: one array of positions and one graphics object per redraw instead of a phx.Body each, so that many thousands of them stay fast. A group holds at most 10 000 particles; use several groups for more.

Particles can be added and removed at any time, also while the simulation runs, and their positions and velocities read and set as N×3 arrays. A particle group is a top-level object: hand it to the simulation like a body.

Creation

Syntax

p = phx.Particles(ax,"Positions",P) p = phx.Particles(___,Name,Value)

Description

p = phx.Particles(ax,"Positions",P) creates particles at the rows of P (N×3) and draws them into the axes ax; [] draws nothing. A group may also start empty and get its particles by add.

Properties

Particles (read when the simulation starts)

PropertyType / DefaultDescription
Radius0.02Radius of every particle, m.
Density1000Density of the particles, kg/m³.
RotationfalseRotation of the particles: false = they only slide, true = they also rotate and roll.

Settings (apply at any time)

PropertyType / DefaultDescription
Friction0.5Friction coefficient; a contact combines the values of both bodies.
Restitution0Restitution (bounciness) coefficient, 0 = no bounce.
LinearDamping0Damping of the linear velocities, dv/dt = −LinearDamping·v, 1/s.
AngularDamping0Damping of the angular velocities of rotating particles, 1/s.
SelfCollisionstrueCollisions of the particles with each other.
SleepingfalseSleeping of particles at rest: a resting heap then costs next to nothing until a body or a force touches it.
MarkerSize6Size of the drawn points, points.
RandomTint0Random shade variation between the particles: each is drawn in Color*(1 - rand*RandomTint), its random number kept for good.
Color1×3 doubleColor of the particles.

State

PositionsN×3 doublePositions of the particle centres in the global frame, m. Setting it during the simulation needs one row per particle; before it, any number of rows replaces the particles.
VelocitiesN×3 doubleVelocities of the particles, m/s.
Countdouble (read-only)Number of particles.
Massdouble (read-only)Mass of one particle, kg.

Object Functions

Examples

Gravel poured into a box

clf; view(3); axis equal; grid on; camlight headlight;

phx.assembly.arena(gca, "Size", [1 1 0.3]);
[x, y, z] = ndgrid(-0.3:0.06:0.3, -0.3:0.06:0.3, 0.3:0.06:0.6);
gravel = phx.Particles(gca, "Positions", [x(:) y(:) z(:)], "Radius", 0.025, ...
    "Density", 1600, "Color", [0.8 0.6 0.4], "RandomTint", 0.4, "MarkerSize", 10);

sim = phx.Simulation(gca, "EngineSettings", phx.engine.Settings("LengthScale", 0.1));
sim.step(2, 400, 4);
