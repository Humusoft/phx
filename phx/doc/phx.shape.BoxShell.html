phx.shape.BoxShell

Box shell shape with a wall of given thickness

Class  phx.shape.BoxShell  |  Superclass: phx.base.Shape

Description

phx.shape.BoxShell is a hollow box: a wall of WallThickness inside the outer Size, solid from both sides like a vessel of sheet metal. A body outside the shell touches its outer face, a body inside it, in the cavity, its inner face: a static shell is a tank or an aquarium for bodies, particles and liquids, a dynamic one a closed crate that can carry a load.

The edges and corners of the cavity are rounded by InnerEdgeRadius, sharp by default, and those outside by InnerEdgeRadius + WallThickness, as a bent sheet is: the wall has the same thickness everywhere. The shell is drawn with that rounding and collides with it.

The collision shape is an ideal box shell, independent of the visual details, built into the engine as phx.shape.SphereShell is, with the same behaviour: it collides with all other shapes, meshes, terrains and other shells included, as cheaply as a box. In the cavity every flat wall a body reaches gives it its own contact, so a ball heading into a corner or a box pushed into one touches every wall it meets. As any thin wall, the shell lets through a body that moves farther in one step than the wall thickness plus its own size; shorten the step for fast bodies. Mass properties are those of the rounded box less its cavity, at the density of the body (Density).

The shape draws its outer face and its inner face, turned to the cavity, their rounded edges with Segments segments per full turn; a texture is projected onto each side. Alpha is the opacity of the wall as a whole: each face is drawn with the opacity 1 - sqrt(1 - Alpha).

Creation

Syntax

shape = phx.shape.BoxShell shape = phx.shape.BoxShell(Name,Value)

Description

shape = phx.shape.BoxShell creates a 1 m cube shell with a 5 cm wall and a sharp cavity. phx.shape.BoxShell(Name,Value) sets properties with name-value arguments.

Properties

Geometry

PropertyType / DefaultDescription
Size[1 1 1]Outer size of the shell [x y z], m; positive. A phx.shape.Box of the same Size has the same outside, its edges sharp.
WallThickness0.05Thickness of the wall, inwards from the outer size, m; positive.
InnerEdgeRadius0Radius of the rounded edges and corners of the cavity, m; 0 keeps them sharp. The outer edges are rounded by this radius plus WallThickness.
InnerSizedouble (dependent, read-only)Size of the cavity, Size - 2*WallThickness, m.
Segments24Number of segments of the rounded edges per full turn.

Appearance properties, Alpha among them, are inherited from phx.base.Shape.

Note. Half of Size must exceed WallThickness + InnerEdgeRadius in every direction. This is checked where the shape is used (drawn, weighed or simulated), not when a property is set, so they may be set in any order; a shape without a cavity is an error (phx:BoxShell:badWallThickness). A solid box is a phx.shape.Box.

Examples

Particles in a see-through aquarium

phx.extra.Viewer("clear", "DefaultCameraPosition", [2.5 -2.5 2]);

phx.Body("Type", "static", "Position", [0 0 0.4], "Shape", ...
    {"BoxShell", "Size", [1.2 0.6 0.8], "WallThickness", 0.02, ...
    "InnerEdgeRadius", 0.01, "Alpha", 0.4});
phx.Particles(gca, "Shape", {"Box", "Size", [0.5 0.4 0.3]}, ...
    "Position", [-0.2 0 0.5], "Radius", 0.02);

sim = phx.Simulation;
sim.step(3, 600, 1);
