phx.shape.CapsuleShell

Capsule shell shape with a wall of given thickness

Class  phx.shape.CapsuleShell  |  Superclass: phx.base.Shape

Description

phx.shape.CapsuleShell is a hollow capsule: a wall of WallThickness inside the outer Diameter, around a cylindrical part of Height along Axis closed by two hemispherical ends, as phx.shape.Capsule has them. The wall is solid from both sides like a vessel of sheet metal: a body outside the shell touches its outer face, a body inside it, in the cavity, its inner face. A static shell is an upright or lying tank for bodies, particles and liquids; a dynamic one lying on its side is a drum that rolls like a cylinder.

The collision shape is an ideal capsule shell, independent of the visual details, built into the engine as phx.shape.SphereShell is, with the same behaviour: it collides with all other shapes, meshes, terrains and other shells included, as cheaply as a capsule; a convex body in the cavity rests on the corners of its face towards the wall, and a capsule shell lying in another shell rests on both its ends. As any thin wall, it lets through a body that moves farther in one step than the wall thickness plus its own size; shorten the step for fast bodies. Mass properties are those of the solid capsule of the outer face less that of the cavity, at the density of the body (Density).

The shape draws its outer face and its inner face, turned to the cavity, each a revolved profile of Segments segments. Alpha is the opacity of the wall as a whole: each face is drawn with the opacity 1 - sqrt(1 - Alpha).

Creation

Syntax

shape = phx.shape.CapsuleShell shape = phx.shape.CapsuleShell(Name,Value)

Description

shape = phx.shape.CapsuleShell creates a shell of 1 m outer diameter around a 1 m cylindrical part along z, with a 5 cm wall. phx.shape.CapsuleShell(Name,Value) sets properties with name-value arguments.

Properties

Geometry

PropertyType / DefaultDescription
Diameter1Outer diameter of the shell, m; positive.
Radiusdouble (dependent)Outer radius, m. Setting it updates Diameter.
Height1Height of the cylindrical part, without the hemispherical ends, m; not negative. Zero is a spherical shell.
Axis"z"Modeling axis: "x", "y" or "z".
WallThickness0.05Thickness of the wall, inwards from the outer diameter, m; positive and less than the outer radius.
InnerDiameterdouble (dependent, read-only)Diameter of the cavity, Diameter - 2*WallThickness, m.
Segments24Number of segments of each face around the axis.

Appearance properties, Alpha among them, are inherited from phx.base.Shape.

Note. WallThickness is checked against the diameter where the shape is used, not when it is set, so the two may be set in any order. A wall as thick as the radius leaves no cavity and is an error (phx:CapsuleShell:badWallThickness); a solid capsule is a phx.shape.Capsule.

Examples

Drum with balls rolling over a floor

phx.extra.Viewer("clear", "DefaultCameraPosition", [3 -3 2]);

phx.Body("Type", "static", "Position", [0 0 -0.05], "Shape", {"Box", "Size", [4 6 0.1]});
drum = phx.Body("Position", [0 -2 0.3], "Shape", {"CapsuleShell", "Diameter", 0.6, ...
    "Height", 1, "Axis", "x", "WallThickness", 0.02, "Alpha", 0.4});
phx.Particles(gca, "Shape", {"Box", "Size", [0.8 0.3 0.15]}, ...
    "Position", [0 -2 0.15], "Radius", 0.03);
drum.LinearVelocity = [0 2 0];

sim = phx.Simulation;
sim.step(3, 600, 1);
