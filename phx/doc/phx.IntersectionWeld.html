phx.IntersectionWeld

Rigid body welded into a soft body where they overlap

Class  phx.IntersectionWeld  |  Superclass: phx.base.Object

Description

phx.IntersectionWeld joins a phx.SoftBody or a phx.FlexibleBody to a phx.Body that is placed so that it overlaps the soft body: every vertex of the soft body that lies inside the body's shape when the simulation starts is welded to the body. The body then holds and loads the soft body like a metal insert held in rubber: joints, motors, springs and forces act on the soft body through it, and the soft body acts back on the body. The two do not collide with each other. One body may be welded into several soft bodies and so join them.

The body should overlap at least three vertices that do not lie on one line, or it cannot pass on a turn, and it should not be much lighter than the vertices it holds. The weld is deleted together with either of its two objects.

Creation

Syntax

weld = phx.IntersectionWeld(softBody,body)

Description

weld = phx.IntersectionWeld(softBody,body) welds body into softBody.

Input Arguments

ArgumentTypeDescription
softBodyphx.SoftBody or phx.FlexibleBody (scalar)The deformable body.
bodyphx.Body (scalar)The rigid body that overlaps it.

Properties

PropertyTypeDescription
VertexCountdouble (read-only)Number of vertices of the soft body welded to the body; 0 before the simulation starts. Fewer than 3 gives a warning as the simulation starts.

Examples

A rubber block hanging from a plate

clf; view(3); axis equal; grid on; camlight headlight;

[x, y, z] = ndgrid(-0.2:0.1:0.2);
V = [x(:) y(:) z(:)];
block = phx.FlexibleBody(gca, "Vertices", V, "Tetrahedra", delaunay(V), ...
    "Position", [0 0 1], "YoungsModulus", 1e5);
plate = phx.Body("Type", "static", "Position", [0 0 1.2], ...
    "Shape", {"Box", "Size", [0.6 0.6 0.05]});
weld = phx.IntersectionWeld(block, plate);    % holds the top layer of vertices

sim = phx.Simulation({plate, block});
sim.step(2, 400, 4);
weld.VertexCount
