phx.Liquid

Liquid of particles (weakly compressible SPH)

Class  phx.Liquid  |  Superclass: phx.base.ParticleGroup

Description

phx.Liquid is a body of liquid made of particles. Unlike phx.Particles, the particles do not collide with each other: the pressure of the liquid keeps them apart instead (smoothed particle hydrodynamics, weakly compressible). The liquid flows, splashes and fills containers; it collides with all bodies, and a dynamic body in it floats and is pushed by it, its weight and the forces of the liquid acting on each other. For a body floating on still water of a flat surface, phx.Buoyancy is far cheaper.

The particles are the same as those of phx.Particles: their centres are the Vertices, in the frame of the liquid that Position and Orientation place (by default the global frame), and so are their Velocities; CurrentVertices and CurrentVelocities are the same in the global frame. They are the state itself, read and set as N×3 arrays before, during and after a run, and a new simulation goes on from where the last one ended. Particles can be added and removed at any time, also while the simulation runs. A liquid is a top-level object: hand it to the simulation like a body. It is drawn as a smooth surface over the particles, or as the particles themselves.

The liquid is explicit in time: the simulation step (the interval over the substeps of sim.step) must stay under 0.4*Spacing/(SoundSpeed + v), v the fastest flow, for example 0.25 ms at a Spacing of 2 cm and a SoundSpeed of 30 m/s. The particles touch the walls with a radius of Spacing/2: give the simulation an engine LengthScale of the order of the Spacing (phx.engine.Settings), or the default contact margin of 4 cm rounds thin walls and lets the liquid into the corners of a tank.

Creation

Syntax

w = phx.Liquid(ax,"Shape",shape) w = phx.Liquid(ax,"Vertices",P) w = phx.Liquid(___,Name,Value)

Description

w = phx.Liquid(ax,"Shape",shape) fills a shape with the liquid, on a cubic lattice of the Spacing, in the frame of the liquid, and draws it into the axes ax; [] draws nothing. The shape is a phx.shape.* object or a cell array of a shape name and its arguments, as phx.Body takes it; Box, Sphere, Cylinder, Cone, Capsule and Tube can be filled. A column of water 0.2 m wide and 0.4 m high on the floor:

water = phx.Liquid(gca, "Shape", {"Box", "Size", [0.2 0.4 0.4]}, ...
    "Position", [-0.5 0 0.2], "Spacing", 0.02, "SoundSpeed", 30);

w = phx.Liquid(ax,"Vertices",P) creates the liquid's particles at the rows of P (N×3, in the frame of the liquid) instead, best on a cubic lattice of the Spacing. A liquid may also start empty and get its particles by add. A shape and Vertices in one constructor call are an error.

Examples

Dam break with a floating crate

clf; view(3); axis equal; grid on; camlight headlight;

phx.assembly.arena(gca, "Size", [0.8 0.3 0.4], "Thickness", 0.04);
water = phx.Liquid(gca, "Shape", {"Box", "Size", [0.2 0.3 0.3]}, "Position", [-0.3 0 0.15]);
phx.Body(gca, "Position", [-0.3 0 0.33], "Density", 400, ...
    "Shape", {"Box", "Size", [0.08 0.08 0.05], "Color", [0.85 0.65 0.4]});

% Contact tolerances for a Spacing of 2 cm, steps of 0.25 ms
sim = phx.Simulation(gca, "EngineSettings", phx.engine.Settings("LengthScale", 0.02));
sim.step(1.5, 6000, 40);

Pressure of water at rest

A tank filled with water, drawn as its particles; after a second the pressures of the particles grow with the depth, close to ρgh.

clf; view(3); axis equal; grid on;

phx.assembly.arena(gca, "Size", [0.2 0.2 0.3], "Thickness", 0.04);
water = phx.Liquid(gca, "Shape", {"Box", "Size", [0.2 0.2 0.2]}, "Position", [0 0 0.1], ...
    "Style", "points", "MarkerSize", 8);

sim = phx.Simulation(gca, "EngineSettings", phx.engine.Settings("LengthScale", 0.02));
sim.step(1, 4000, 40);

z = water.CurrentVertices(:, 3);
h = max(z) + water.Spacing/2 - z;
figure; plot(water.Pressures, z, ".", water.Density*9.81*h, z, "-");
xlabel("Pressure (Pa)"); ylabel("Height (m)"); legend("Particles", "\rho g h");
