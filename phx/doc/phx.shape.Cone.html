phx.shape.Cone

Cone or truncated cone shape

Class  phx.shape.Cone  |  Superclass: phx.base.Shape

Description

phx.shape.Cone is a cone or a truncated cone. The visual appearance is a revolved profile with an adjustable number of segments; the collision shape and mass properties are modeled as an ideal cone independent of the visual detail.

Diameter gives the diameters at the negative and the positive end of Axis. A single value d is [d 0], a sharp cone with its apex on the positive axis; two values make a truncated cone, or a cylinder when they are equal. Either way the property reads back as a pair, and Radius follows it.

Creation

Syntax

shape = phx.shape.Cone shape = phx.shape.Cone(Name,Value)

Description

shape = phx.shape.Cone creates a unit cone with default parameters. phx.shape.Cone(Name,Value) sets properties with name-value arguments.

Properties

Geometry

PropertyType / DefaultDescription
Diameter[1 0]Diameters at the negative and the positive end of Axis, m. A single value d is [d 0]; the values must not be negative, nor both zero.
Radiusdouble (dependent)Radii at the two ends, m, as Diameter. Setting it updates Diameter.
Height1Cone height, m.
Axis"z"Modeling axis: "x", "y" or "z".
Segments24Number of segments around the base.
Density1000Volumetric density, kg/m³.

Appearance properties are inherited from phx.base.Shape.

Note. The origin of the shape is at the middle of its height, not at its centre of mass, which lies closer to the wider end: a quarter of the height below the origin for a sharp cone. The body gets it as its CenterOfMass and turns about it.

Examples

Simple falling cone

phx.extra.Viewer("clear", "DefaultCameraPosition", [10 10 10]);

c = phx.Body("Position", [0 0 2], ...
    "Shape", {"Cone", "Diameter", 1, "Height", 1.5});

sim = phx.Simulation;
sim.step(3, 300, 1);

Truncated cone standing on its wide end

phx.extra.Viewer("clear", "DefaultCameraPosition", [6 6 4]);

phx.Body("Type", "static", "Position", [0 0 -0.05], ...
    "Shape", {"Box", "Size", [3 3 0.1]});
phx.Body("Position", [0 0 1], ...
    "Shape", {"Cone", "Diameter", [1 0.5], "Height", 0.8});

sim = phx.Simulation;
sim.step(2, 200, 1);
